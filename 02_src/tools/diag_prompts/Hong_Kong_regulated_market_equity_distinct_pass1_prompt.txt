Tier: Chapter 19C Secondary Listing of Overseas Issuers

[secondary_admission]
  description: Scope: Sets additional requirements, modifications or exceptions for overseas issuers seeking or having a secondary listing in Hong Kong (Rule 19C.02 / 19C.02A scope statements depending on version). Listing on the issuer’s primary exchange must have been granted before Hong Kong listing (Rule 19C.02A(2)); the Exchange may refuse listing under specified circumstances (Rule 19C.02A(1)). Overseas issuers must appoint/maintain an authorised service recipient in Hong Kong and comply with Hong Kong securities registration requirements (Rules 19.05(2)–(5) applied via 19C.02A(3)–(4)). Eligibility/qualification thresholds (without WVR): Two alternative criteria in Rule 19C.05A: Criteria A: Track record of good regulatory compliance ≥ 5 full financial years on a Qualifying Exchange (or any Recognised Stock Exchange for non-Greater China issuers without WVR and without Greater China centre of gravity) and expected market capitalisation ≥ HK$3,000,000,000 at listing. Criteria B: Track record of good regulatory compliance ≥ 2 full financial years on a Qualifying Exchange and expected market capitalisation ≥ HK$10,000,000,000 at listing. A waiver of the track record may be granted if the applicant is well-established and has much larger market capitalisation than HK$10,000,000,000. Eligibility/qualification thresholds (with WVR): If the issuer has a WVR structure, it must meet: (i) track record of good regulatory compliance ≥ 2 full financial years on a Qualifying Exchange (Rule 19C.04); and (ii) either expected market capitalisation ≥ HK$40,000,000,000 at listing or expected market capitalisation ≥ HK$10,000,000,000 with revenue ≥ HK$1,000,000,000 for the most recent audited financial year (Rule 19C.05). Shareholder protection: Appendix 3/13 shareholder protection standards are modified for Non-Greater China Issuers and Grandfathered Greater China Issuers; they must ensure equivalent shareholder protections specified in Rule 19C.07 and demonstrate how their domestic laws and constitutional documents provide them (Rules 19C.06–19C.09). Ongoing compliance with these standards is required (Rule 19C.09).
  source: Chapter 19C (Overseas Issuers version) PDF — Rules 19C.02A(1)–(4), 19C.04–19C.05, 19C.05A; Chapter 19C (Qualifying Issuers version) PDF — Rules 19C.06–19C.10 concerning shareholder protection standards.

[instrument_requirements]

[restrictions_and_lock_ups]
  description: Automatic waivers for secondary listings: Numerous continuing obligations and restrictions are dis-applied, including controlling shareholder lock-up (Rule 10.07) and 6-month post-listing issuance restrictions (Rule 10.08), as listed in Rule 19C.11 (Overseas Issuers version). Migration of trading: If the majority of trading (≥55% of total worldwide dollar trading volume over the most recent financial year) in a secondary listed issuer’s shares migrates to HKEX’s markets on a permanent basis, the issuer will be regarded as having a dual-primary listing; the automatic waivers cease after a 12-month grace period (Rule 19C.13 and Notes 1–2), with a 3-year grandfathering for continuing transactions in place at the notice date (Note 3). If the issuer ceases to be listed on its primary Recognised Stock Exchange, HKEX will regard it as having a primary listing in Hong Kong and the waivers also cease, with limited exemptions for pre-existing continuing transactions (Rule 19C.13A and note).
