[instrument_requirements]
  description: Minimum market capitalisation at listing (issuer): Expected market capitalisation of the applicant at listing must be at least HK$500,000,000, calculated on all issued shares (including unlisted/other market-listed classes) at listing (Rule 8.09(2)). Minimum market capitalisation per class: For each class of securities to be listed (other than options/warrants), expected market cap at listing must be at least HK$50,000,000 (Rule 8.09(3)). For options/warrants, at least HK$10,000,000 (Rule 8.09(4)). Minimum market capitalisation of public float: The securities held by the public (as defined by Rule 8.24) must have expected market capitalisation at listing of at least HK$125,000,000 (Rule 8.09(1)). Public float percentage and calculation: At least 25% of the issuer’s total number of issued shares must be held by the public at all times (Rule 8.08(1)(a)). If the issuer has other classes already listed/issued elsewhere, total securities held by the public across all regulated markets must be at least 25% of total issued shares, but the class to be listed in Hong Kong must be at least 15% of total issued shares with expected market cap of not less than HK$125,000,000 (Rule 8.08(1)(b)). The Exchange may accept a lower public float between 15% and 25% for issuers with expected market cap at listing over HK$10,000,000,000 if distribution supports proper market operation; must disclose and confirm sufficiency in annual reports (Rule 8.08(1)(d)). If public float falls below 15% (or 10% if a 8.08(1)(d) waiver was granted), trading is normally suspended until restored (Note (1) to Rule 8.08(1)). Number and spread of shareholders: For a class new to listing, there must be an adequate spread; in all cases at least 300 shareholders (Rule 8.08(2)). Not more than 50% of the securities in public hands at listing may be beneficially owned by the three largest public shareholders (Rule 8.08(3)). Definition/exclusions for ‘public’: Core connected persons are not public, nor persons financed by or taking instructions from core connected persons regarding acquisition, disposal, voting or other disposition (Rule 8.24). ‘Adequate market’ and ‘open market’: Issuer must demonstrate sufficient public interest in the business and securities (Rule 8.07) and meet open market distribution/float tests (Rule 8.08). Transferability and CCASS eligibility: Securities must be freely transferable (Rule 8.13) and must be ‘Eligible Securities’ in CCASS from first day of dealings (Rule 8.13A), unless waived for legal transferability issues; issuer must ensure they remain Eligible Securities. Minimum issue price: No explicit fixed minimum share price specified; expected issue price is used to determine market value of other un/overseas-listed classes in market capitalisation calculations (Rule 8.09A).

  description: Core eligibility routes (Chapter 8): An issuer must satisfy one of the three financial eligibility tests under Rule 8.05: 1) Profit Test (Rule 8.05(1)) — 3-year track record with minimum profits: HK$35,000,000 in the most recent year and HK$45,000,000 in aggregate for the two preceding years; management continuity for 3 years; ownership continuity and control for the most recent audited financial year.
