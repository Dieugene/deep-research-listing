[instrument_requirements]
  description: Minimum nominal amount (issue size): Rule 23.08 — at least HK$50,000,000 nominal for each class seeking listing. Exceptions/alternatives: further issues fungible with already listed class are not subject to this limit; in exceptional cases, a lower minimum nominal amount may be acceptable where Exchange is satisfied as to marketability. For options/warrants to subscribe/purchase debt, same limits apply as for the underlying debt to be subscribed/purchased. Calculation/verification: based on nominal amount per class at time of listing; fungibility assessed by uniformity in all respects with listed class; Exchange discretion for marketability-based exceptions. Variations for special issuers: Chapters 31 and 32 apply the same HK$50m minimum nominal to States and Supranationals (31.02(1), 32.02(1)), with similar exceptional-case discretion. Authorisation and legal conformity: Rule 23.10 — must conform to issuer’s law and constitutional documents; all authorisations needed must have been given. Paying agent: Rule 23.12 — issuer must maintain a paying agent at a Hong Kong address until no debt security is outstanding, unless it performs that function itself. Options/warrants attached: Rule 23.11 — must also comply with requirements applicable to options/warrants (see Ch. 15 or 27 as appropriate). Dependence: structure and rights of options/warrants determine applicable chapter. [eligibility_requirements]
  description: Valid incorporation/establishment and due authorisation to commence business: Rule 23.03. Freshness of accounts: Rule 23.07 — for new applicants, the latest financial period reported by reporting accountants must not have ended more than nine months before the date of the listing document. Guarantor eligibility: Retail regime allows guaranteed issues; the guarantor must meet the same audited accounts and shareholders’ funds requirements if its shares are not listed (Rules 23.06–23.08) and its guarantee must be properly authorised and lawful (Rule 23.10 mutatis mutandis). State and supranational guarantees subject to modified chapters for content/eligibility. Asset-backed securities: Retail regime allows ABS with additional conditions under Ch. 23 (as reproduced in the consolidated section): Single purpose undertaking; may add further assets and issue further classes backed by separate asset pools; Rules under the “asset-backed securities” subsection following 23.10 in the consolidated Debt Securities section; exceptions: 23.06 and 23.07 do not apply to ABS; backing equity securities must be listed/traded on a regulated market and represent non-controlling interests; trustee/independent party required with access rights to information. [common_monitoring_common]
  description: The GEM Listing Rules apply specifically to GEM, with debt securities governed by Chapters 26-35. Chapter 30 covers "Debt issues to professional investors only" (PI-only debt), and Chapter 31 addresses continuing obligations for other debt securities. Rule 2.25 outlines this structure, and Rule 2.11 mandates that a company cannot list debt on GEM unless its own or its holding company's equity is, or will be, simultaneously listed on GEM, which ensures the cross-application of relevant equity disclosure and inside information obligations.

Post-admission monitoring tools applicable across all GEM debt include:
- Exchange enquiry and issuer prompt response: Issuers must respond promptly to Exchange enquiries regarding unusual price/volume movements, potential false markets, or other matters (Chapter 30 Rule 30.39A). They are also required to make announcements to prevent or correct a false market (Rule 30.40) and to disclose information materially affecting their ability to meet obligations (Rule 30.40A). If inside information under SFO Part XIVA is involved, a simultaneous GEM announcement and notification to the Exchange of any SFC waiver application are necessary (Rule 30.40B). If such information cannot be announced promptly, the issuer must apply for a trading halt/suspension (Rule 30.40C). Quarterly development announcements are required after a suspension (Rule 30.40D).
- Event-driven announcement thresholds: Defaults, appointment/application for receiver/manager, winding-up petitions/orders/provisional liquidators, and members'/creditors' voluntary winding-up resolutions must be announced as soon as reasonably practicable (Rule 30.40E). Aggregate redemptions/cancellations exceeding 10% of an issue, and every subsequent 5% interval, must be announced "as soon as possible" (Rule 30.41(a)). Any public disclosure on another stock exchange concerning the issuer's listed debt must also be announced (Rule 30.41(b)).
- Direct reporting/notifications to Exchange (beyond public disclosure): Issuers must provide advance notification of proposals to replace the trustee, amend the trust deed, or amend terms of convertible listed debt, and must not proceed until the Exchange advises on potential conditions (Rule 30.42). They must notify the Exchange as soon as possible of full cancellation/redemption/conversion of an issue and apply for delisting (Rule 30.43). Notification is also required as soon as possible if the listed debt is listed elsewhere (Rule 30.44). Copies of any circulars sent to bondholders or trustees must be provided to the Exchange (website notification is acceptable in lieu of a copy) (Rule 30.45). Annual accounts and interim reports must be provided to the Exchange when issued (or website publication notified), with provisions for guarantor substitution if guaranteed by a corporate guarantor (Rule 30.46). Issuers must maintain two authorised representatives to communicate with the Exchange and notify any changes (Rule 30.47).
- Publication mechanics: When a GEM debt issuer is required to make an announcement, it must do so via a GEM announcement as per Rules 16.17 and 16.18. The announcement can be in English or Chinese only and must include the prescribed Exchange disclaimer text (Rule 30.38).

Responsible bodies:
- The Listing Division is the primary administrator and enforcer. The GEM Listing Committee holds reserved powers, including cancellation, and delegates operational decisions. The Executive Director of the Listing Division is authorized to approve listing applications for Chapter 30 (PI-only) debt (Rule 3.05A). The GEM Listing Committee retains the power to cancel a listing (Rule 3.09). The SFC has an oversight role, approving rule changes (GEM Rule 2.08) and potentially requesting reviews of disciplinary decisions (Rule 3.17A). Inside information obligations fall under SFO Part XIVA, with enforcement handled by the SFC/Market Misconduct Tribunal (MMT), separate from Exchange disciplinary processes.

Sanctions and disciplinary framework (Exchange-wide for GEM):
- Jurisdiction: Rule 3.10 specifies parties subject to Exchange discipline, including listed issuers and their subsidiaries, directors and senior management, substantial or significant shareholders, professional advisers, authorised representatives, and, for guaranteed debt, the guarantor.
- Available sanctions (Rule 3.11): private reprimand, public criticism, public censure, prejudice statement and director unsuitability statement, denial of market facilities (withholding Exchange approvals), trading suspension, cancellation of listing, banning professional advisers/individuals from appearing before the Listing Division/Committee, reporting to the SFC/other authorities, rectification/remedial orders within a stipulated period, and "such other action" as the Exchange deems fit, including publicizing any action. Monetary fines are not listed as available sanctions for GEM issuers/directors in Rule 3.11.
- Publication policy: Public sanctions are published "with reasons." For private reprimands, the substance may be published "with reasons" without disclosing identities (Rule 3.11 Notes 1-2). The Exchange maintains a public database of disciplinary sanctions.
- Process and rights: Parties have the right to written reasons and a review by the GEM Listing Review Committee. A review request must be made within 7 business days of the decision or issuance of written reasons; a request for written reasons must be made within 3 business days, with reasons provided within 14 business days (Rules 3.12-3.14). Parties have the right to attend, make submissions, and be accompanied by advisers; the Listing Division must provide its papers in advance (Rule 3.17). The SFC may request a review of a disciplinary decision, and if the decision is overturned/modified/varied on SFC's review, the relevant party has a further and final right to a second Listing Review Committee review (Rule 3.17A). Follow-on measures (e.g., denial of facilities, suspension/cancellation) may be imposed if a sanctioned individual remains as director/senior management after the specified date (Rule 3.11A).

Enforcement approach and monitoring:
- The Exchange employs a prevention-detection-correction framework for rule enforcement, which includes market surveillance/monitoring, enquiries, on-site inspections, and an Annual Attestation & Inspection Programme for participants. While this program targets market participants, it reflects HKEX's broader proactive monitoring and corrective action posture. For issuers, the Listing Division's monitoring primarily involves continuous disclosure review, Exchange enquiries, trading halts/suspensions, and disciplinary actions under Chapter 3.
